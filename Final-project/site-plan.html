The Cozy Nook

Site Planning Document | WDD 231 Individual Project

1. Site Name & Identification

Site Name: The Cozy Nook

Reason for Selection: The name evokes a warm, comfortable, and welcoming environment tailored for passionate readers. It captures the spirit of curling up with a mysterious thriller or a charming romance story, establishing an atmospheric brand for book discovery and goal tracking.

Optional Domain Availability: thecozynookreads.org

2. Site Purpose

The Cozy Nook serves as an atmospheric digital hub for readers who enjoy mystery, thriller, and romance novels. The site provides a curated discovery platform where users can dynamically filter books by genre, explore detailed plot overlays, track personal reading goals, and maintain a client-side reading log stored safely in their browser.

3. Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "Where can I find cozy mystery and romantic suspense recommendations filtered specifically by genre, along with character descriptions before I decide to read?"
  • Scenario 2: "How can I log books I've recently completed, rate them out of five stars, and keep a personal reading list that saves automatically on my device?"

6. Landing Page (index.html) Wireframe Plan

Below is the visual structure outline for small (mobile) and large (desktop) viewports.

Small Viewport (Mobile: ~320px - 767px)

  • Header: Brand logo/name + Hamburger toggle button.
  • Nav: Vertical slide-out list (Home, Library, Tracker).
  • Hero Section: Stacked banner image with welcoming headline and single call-to-action button ("Explore Library").
  • Book of the Month: Single centered card layout with book cover stacked above title, badge, and summary.
  • Genre Highlights: 1-column stacked cards linking to genre filters.
  • Footer: Social icons, student copyright, video demo link, and attributions link stacked vertically.

Large Viewport (Desktop: 768px+)

  • Header: Logo on left + horizontal inline navigation links on right with active wayfinding state.
  • Hero Section: 2-column split layout (Left: Engaging copy & CTA; Right: High-resolution featured book graphic).
  • Book of the Month: Horizontal card layout (Left: Large book cover; Right: Details, rating stars, and modal trigger button).
  • Genre Highlights: 3-column responsive grid (Mystery, Romance, Thriller).
  • Footer: Multi-column footer layout (Navigation shortcuts, project metadata, video demo, attributions).
